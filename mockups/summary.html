Workers AI Now Runs Large Models: Powering the Next Generation of AI Agents
Published on March 19, 2026
Prerequisites
Anycast Routing BGP DNS Resolution
Tags
ai infrastructure machine-learning distributed-systems

Cloudflare has announced that Workers AI now supports large language models, enabling developers to run powerful AI models directly on Cloudflare's global network. This update significantly expands the capabilities available to developers building AI-powered applications and agents at the edge.

The key innovation is Cloudflare's optimized inference stack, which reduces the cost and latency of running large models by distributing inference workloads across their global infrastructure. This means developers can now build sophisticated AI agents that operate closer to end users, without the overhead of managing their own GPU infrastructure.

The announcement includes support for Kimi K2.5 and other frontier models, with Cloudflare positioning Workers AI as the ideal platform for running autonomous agents that require fast, reliable, and cost-effective model inference at scale.
